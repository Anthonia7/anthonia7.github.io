01

Healthcare Patient Analysis

Excel · PivotTables

Exploratory analysis of 54,966 patient records to uncover patterns across medical conditions, demographics, admissions, billing, length of stay, medications, insurance providers and test results.

View case study

1. Introduction

This project explores a healthcare patient dataset to identify meaningful patterns across patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, billing, medications, insurance providers and test results. The analysis transforms raw patient records into clear findings that are easier to understand and communicate through an Excel dashboard.

2. Problem Statement

Healthcare organizations collect large volumes of patient information, but raw records can make it difficult to identify important trends quickly. This project focuses on answering practical questions around common medical conditions, admission patterns, healthcare billing, hospital stays, medications, insurance coverage and test results.

3. Data Sourcing

The analysis uses a healthcare patient dataset containing 54,966 records across 20 columns. The dataset includes patient demographics, medical conditions, admission information, medication, test results, insurance providers, billing amounts and length of stay.

4. Data Transformation & Cleaning

The dataset was prepared in Excel before analysis. The cleaning process included reviewing the dataset structure, checking for missing or inconsistent values, checking for duplicate records, standardizing date formats and reviewing data types. The cleaned data was then organized for analysis using Excel PivotTables.

5. Data Modelling

Because this project was completed in Excel, a complex relational data model was not required. The cleaned patient dataset was maintained as a structured table, with fields organized so they could be summarized and compared through PivotTables, filters and calculated values.

6. Analytics & Measures

PivotTables and Excel calculations were used to summarize the dataset and answer the project’s business questions.

  • Total patient records
  • Patient distribution by medical condition
  • Patient distribution by admission type
  • Average billing amount
  • Maximum length of stay
  • Medication frequency
  • Insurance provider distribution
  • Test result distribution
  • Patient demographic patterns

7. Dashboard & Visuals

The dashboard brings the analysis together through KPI summaries, charts and category comparisons. It provides an overview of patient demographics, medical conditions, admission types, billing, length of stay, medications, insurance providers and test results, allowing key patterns to be identified at a glance.

8. Insights & Findings

  • 54,966 patient records were analyzed.
  • Arthritis was the most frequently recorded condition with 9,218 patients, followed by Asthma with 9,095 patients.
  • The average billing amount was approximately 25,544.31.
  • The maximum recorded length of stay was 15 days.
  • Elective admissions were the most common admission type.
  • Lipitor was the most frequently recorded medication.
  • Cigna was the most represented insurance provider in the analysis.
  • Approximately 33.54% of recorded test results were abnormal.

9. Recommendations

  • Monitor frequently recorded conditions such as arthritis and asthma to support healthcare resource planning.
  • Review billing patterns to better understand healthcare cost distribution and identify areas for further investigation.
  • Use admission and length-of-stay patterns to support hospital capacity and operational planning.
  • Monitor abnormal test-result patterns alongside medical conditions and demographics for deeper analysis.
  • Track medication patterns to support medication planning and inventory decisions.
  • Further analysis could examine relationships between medical conditions, medications, billing, length of stay and patient outcomes.

10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how a large healthcare dataset can be cleaned, organized and analyzed in Excel to produce practical insights. By examining 54,966 patient records, the analysis identified patterns across conditions, admissions, billing, medications, insurance, test results and hospital stays. The resulting dashboard turns these findings into a clear visual summary that can support healthcare-related operational analysis and decision-making.

02

E-commerce Sales Analysis

SQL · Power BI

Analysis of a small e-commerce business to understand sales performance, order fulfillment, product categories and customer contribution.

View case study

1. Introduction

This project analyzes transactional data from a small e-commerce business to understand sales performance, order fulfillment, product performance and customer purchasing behavior. SQL was used to work with the relational data, while Power BI was used to model, analyze and visualize the results.

2. Problem Statement

The business has customer, product and order information stored across separate tables, making it difficult to get a clear view of overall performance. This project brings the data together to understand revenue, product categories, order fulfillment and customer contribution.

3. Data Sourcing

The dataset consists of three related tables — Customers, Products and Orders — representing a fictional e-commerce business. The period covered is June 2023 to October 2023, with 80 customers and 130 orders across Electronics, Accessories and Office.

4. Data Transformation & Cleaning

The data was loaded into MySQL and prepared for analysis before being connected to Power BI through an ODBC connection. The tables were reviewed for missing values, duplicates and inconsistencies. No major cleanup was required.

5. Data Modelling

Orders was used as the central fact table, with Customers and Products providing supporting information. Relationships were established using customer_id and product_id.

6. Analytics & Measures

  • Revenue
  • Total Orders
  • Total Customers
  • Average Order Value
  • All Orders
  • Shipped or Completed
  • Completed Orders
  • Top 10 Revenue Share

7. Dashboard & Visuals

The dashboard contains two pages. Page 1 presents Revenue, Orders, Customers, Average Order Value, Revenue by Category, Orders by Status, Revenue by Month and an Order Fulfillment Funnel. Page 2 focuses on the Top 10 Customers by Spend and Top 10 Revenue Share. Slicers allow filtering by Month, Country and Category.

8. Insights & Findings

  • 130 orders from 80 customers generated $6,757.05 in revenue.
  • Average order value was $51.98.
  • Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating category.
  • Monthly revenue rose from $757 in June to a peak of $1,747 in July.
  • 103 of 130 orders (79.2%) were shipped or completed.
  • 77 of 130 orders (59.2%) were completed.
  • 27 orders remained pending.
  • The top 10 customers generated $2,147.53 (31.78%) of total revenue.
  • Anna Mueller was the highest-spending customer at $241.96.

9. Recommendations

  • Investigate the pending orders to improve fulfillment and customer experience.
  • Continue monitoring Electronics because of its strong revenue contribution.
  • Develop retention strategies for high-value customers.
  • Monitor monthly sales while considering incomplete reporting periods such as October.

10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow, from working with relational data in SQL to creating DAX measures and an interactive Power BI dashboard. The analysis provides a clear view of sales performance, product categories, order fulfillment and customer contribution.
