01

Healthcare Analysis

Excel

Analysis of 54,966 patient records to understand medical conditions, demographics, billing, admissions, length of stay, medications, insurance providers and test results.

View case study

1. Introduction

This project analyzes healthcare patient data to identify patterns in patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, billing, length of stay, medications, and test results. Using Excel, the analysis turns a large patient dataset into practical insights that can help understand patient characteristics and healthcare service patterns.

2. Problem Statement

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of patient data, but raw data can make it difficult to identify important patterns. This project focuses on understanding common medical conditions, hospital admissions, billing, medications and test results across the patient population.

3. Data Sourcing

The dataset contains 54,966 patient records across 20 fields, covering patient demographics, medical conditions, hospital admissions, billing, medications and test results.

4. Data Transformation & Cleaning

The dataset was prepared and analyzed in Excel. Date fields were standardized, and the data was reviewed for inconsistencies and formatting issues. Excel functions, sorting, filtering and data preparation techniques were used to make the dataset suitable for analysis.

5. Data Modelling

Because this project was completed entirely in Excel, the patient data was organized into a structured dataset that could be filtered and summarized efficiently using pivot tables and calculated fields.

6. Analytics & Measures

  • Patient count by medical condition
  • Average billing amount
  • Length of hospital stay
  • Admission type distribution
  • Medication frequency
  • Insurance provider distribution
  • Test result distribution

7. Dashboard & Visuals

The Excel analysis presents summary tables, charts and visual comparisons covering medical conditions, patient demographics, billing, admission types, length of stay, medications, insurance providers and test results.

8. Insights & Findings

  • Arthritis was the most common condition, with 9,218 patients.
  • Asthma followed with 9,095 patients.
  • Average billing was approximately $25,544.31.
  • Maximum recorded length of stay was 15 days.
  • Elective admissions were the most common admission type.
  • Lipitor was the most frequently recorded medication.
  • Cigna was the most represented insurance provider.
  • Approximately 33.54% of test results were abnormal.

9. Recommendations

  • Monitor frequently occurring medical conditions to understand areas of high patient demand.
  • Review billing patterns to identify significant differences in healthcare costs.
  • Examine factors associated with longer hospital stays.
  • Track abnormal test results to identify patterns that may require further investigation.

10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Excel to transform a large healthcare dataset into meaningful business and operational insights across patient conditions, demographics, billing, admissions, medications, insurance, length of stay and test results.

02

E-commerce Sales Analysis

SQL · Power BI

Analysis of a small e-commerce business to understand sales performance, order fulfillment, product categories and customer contribution.

View case study

1. Introduction

This project analyzes transactional data from a small e-commerce business to understand sales performance, order fulfillment, product performance and customer purchasing behavior. SQL was used to work with the relational data, while Power BI was used to model, analyze and visualize the results.

2. Problem Statement

The business has customer, product and order information stored across separate tables, making it difficult to get a clear view of overall performance. This project brings the data together to understand revenue, product categories, order fulfillment and customer contribution.

3. Data Sourcing

The dataset consists of three related tables — Customers, Products and Orders — representing a fictional e-commerce business. The period covered is June 2023 to October 2023, with 80 customers and 130 orders across Electronics, Accessories and Office.

4. Data Transformation & Cleaning

The data was loaded into MySQL and prepared for analysis before being connected to Power BI through an ODBC connection. The tables were reviewed for missing values, duplicates and inconsistencies. No major cleanup was required.

5. Data Modelling

Orders was used as the central fact table, with Customers and Products providing supporting information. Relationships were established using customer_id and product_id.

6. Analytics & Measures

  • Revenue
  • Total Orders
  • Total Customers
  • Average Order Value
  • All Orders
  • Shipped or Completed
  • Completed Orders
  • Top 10 Revenue Share

7. Dashboard & Visuals

The dashboard contains two pages. Page 1 presents Revenue, Orders, Customers, Average Order Value, Revenue by Category, Orders by Status, Revenue by Month and an Order Fulfillment Funnel. Page 2 focuses on the Top 10 Customers by Spend and Top 10 Revenue Share. Slicers allow filtering by Month, Country and Category.

8. Insights & Findings

  • 130 orders from 80 customers generated $6,757.05 in revenue.
  • Average order value was $51.98.
  • Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating category.
  • Monthly revenue rose from $757 in June to a peak of $1,747 in July.
  • 103 of 130 orders (79.2%) were shipped or completed.
  • 77 of 130 orders (59.2%) were completed.
  • 27 orders remained pending.
  • The top 10 customers generated $2,147.53 (31.78%) of total revenue.
  • Anna Mueller was the highest-spending customer at $241.96.

9. Recommendations

  • Investigate the pending orders to improve fulfillment and customer experience.
  • Continue monitoring Electronics because of its strong revenue contribution.
  • Develop retention strategies for high-value customers.
  • Monitor monthly sales while considering incomplete reporting periods such as October.

10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow, from working with relational data in SQL to creating DAX measures and an interactive Power BI dashboard. The analysis provides a clear view of sales performance, product categories, order fulfillment and customer contribution.
