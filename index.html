01 · HEALTHCARE

Healthcare Patient Analysis

Exploratory analysis of 54,966 patient records to uncover patterns across medical conditions, demographics, admissions, billing, length of stay, medications, insurance providers and test results.

ExcelPivotTablesHealthcare Analytics
View project ↓
Open case study

Business questions

  • Which medical conditions appear most frequently?
  • What are the common admission patterns?
  • What do billing, length of stay, medication, insurance and test-result patterns show?

Key findings

  • Arthritis was recorded for 9,218 patients, followed by Asthma with 9,095.
  • Average billing was approximately 25,544.31.
  • Maximum length of stay was 15 days.
  • Elective admissions were the most common admission type.
  • Lipitor was the most frequently recorded medication.
  • Approximately 33.54% of recorded test results were abnormal.

Approach

The dataset was prepared and reviewed in Excel, then summarized with PivotTables and dashboard visuals.

Key findings

  • Total revenue was approximately $1.94 million.
  • Total COGS was approximately $722,370, producing gross profit of about $1.22 million.
  • Gross margin was approximately 62.7%.
  • The Original Mr. Fuzzy generated the largest share of product revenue.
  • Refund activity was concentrated in Mr. Fuzzy, with 17 refunds recorded.

Approach

The relational e-commerce data was queried in SQL, cleaned and prepared for analysis, then visualized in Power BI to communicate sales and profitability performance.
